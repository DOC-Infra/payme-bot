# The Appropriation is advisory: PayMe warns but never blocks

PayMe never refuses a payment, approval, Share or Project funding because it would take a month over its Appropriation or a Spending Category over its target. It warns Leadership at the moment of the action, with how far over it would be, and then lets them go ahead. We chose this because Approved work is owed to Employees regardless of the Appropriation, and a hard stop would push payments outside PayMe, leaving its records less complete than the account.

## Consequences

- Warnings appear only to Leadership, when marking a Payout or Disbursement paid and when funding a Project. Requesters never see Appropriation figures.
- Overspending is visible after the fact in `/appropriation` and its report, which is where Leadership explains it to the granting authority.
